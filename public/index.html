Business Problem

Issue

Manual Query Handling

Support staff manually collect customer details, analyse requests, and prepare responses across disconnected systems.

Impact

Slow and Inconsistent Service

Customers experience delayed replies, repeated explanations, and different answers from different staff.

Goal

Agentic AI Workflow

Use AI agents to classify queries, retrieve knowledge, track status, and recommend consistent responses.

A1: Key Decisions

1. Which queries to automate?

Start with repetitive FAQ-style requests such as account information, card issues, application status, and general banking questions.

2. Which systems to integrate?

Connect online forms, email intake, customer record lookup, ticket status, and knowledge base retrieval.

3. When to escalate?

Escalate high-risk, low-confidence, complaint, fraud, or policy-sensitive cases to human staff.

A2: Corrective Actions

AI Query Triage Agent

Classifies incoming messages, identifies intent and urgency, and routes the case to the correct queue automatically.

RAG Knowledge Agent

Retrieves approved FAQ and policy information before generating a response, improving consistency and reducing staff effort.

Status Tracking Dashboard

Shows open, pending, escalated, and resolved cases so staff and managers can monitor real-time progress.

A3: Root Cause Technique — 5 Whys

  1. Why are customers waiting too long? Staff take time to check multiple systems manually.
  2. Why must staff check multiple systems? Customer information and query history are disconnected.
  3. Why are responses inconsistent? Staff use different sources or personal judgement.
  4. Why is status tracking weak? Follow-ups are recorded manually.
  5. Root cause: the workflow lacks integrated automation, standard knowledge retrieval, and real-time tracking.

A4: Controlled Testing Plan

Step 1: Pilot Dataset

Use sample banking queries, FAQs, and mock customer records in a controlled test environment.

Step 2: Human Review

Compare AI responses against approved answers and staff judgement before customer release.

Step 3: Limited Rollout

Deploy to one support team or one query category, monitor issues, then improve prompts and rules.
